# Solaris — Solar Energy Solutions Website

## Overview

Solaris is a responsive website concept for a solar energy business. It explains how solar energy solutions can help households and businesses explore renewable electricity, understand their potential energy savings, and learn how to get started with a solar system.

The website is designed to make solar energy information easier to understand and to give visitors a clear starting point for exploring solar solutions.

## The Real-World Problem

Many households and businesses depend on conventional electricity and may face rising energy costs, uncertainty about long-term expenses, and limited understanding of how solar power could fit their needs. People interested in solar energy may also find it difficult to compare solution types, understand the installation process, or estimate whether a solar system could be worthwhile for them.

A clear, accessible online experience can help visitors learn about available options and make more informed decisions before contacting a solar provider.

## Why This Website Is Needed

Solaris brings key information and interactive tools together in one place. Instead of requiring visitors to search through disconnected sources, it introduces common solar solutions, explains the basic process, offers an illustrative savings calculator, and provides a contact form for the next step.

The website is an informational and demonstration project. It does not claim to solve electricity supply issues directly or replace a professional site assessment.

## How It Works

1. **Explore solar solutions:** Visitors learn about residential systems, commercial solar installations, and energy storage.
2. **Understand the process:** A step-by-step section outlines the typical journey from exploring solar options to installation.
3. **View example energy data:** The dashboard illustrates solar production, household usage, battery status, and energy-flow charts using simulated values.
4. **Estimate potential savings:** Visitors enter details into the calculator to see an illustrative estimate. The result is intended for early exploration, not as a quote or guarantee.
5. **Contact the business:** Visitors can use the contact form to prepare an inquiry and take the next step toward getting professional advice.

## Important Limitations

- **Demo dashboard:** The displayed energy readings are simulated and are not connected to real solar panels, meters, or monitoring hardware.
- **Illustrative calculator:** Estimated savings depend on assumptions and user inputs. Actual results vary with location, sunlight, electricity tariffs, system size, equipment, installation costs, and energy usage.
- **Contact form:** The form is a front-end demonstration. It does not save inquiries to a database or provide a full business CRM workflow; message handling depends on the current client-side implementation.
- **External assets:** Google Fonts and image assets may require an internet connection.
